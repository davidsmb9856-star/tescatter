PANDUAN ODDS MINES & PLUS

✅ ODDS PLUS (DECIMAL ≥ 1.00) Masukkan MODAL. Total hasil = Modal × Odd.
Contoh: Modal Rp 100.000, Odd 1.90 → Hasil Rp 190.000, Profit Rp 90.000.
✅ ODDS PLUS (MALAY 0 < x < 1) Odd 0.80 dikonversi ke decimal 1 + 0.80 = 1.80. Modal tetap.
⚠️ ODDS MINES (NEGATIF) Masukkan TARGET PROFIT. Yang harus dibayar = Target × |Odd|.
Odd -1.33, target Rp 10.000 → bayar 10.000 × 1.33 = Rp 13.300.
Odd -1.42, target Rp 10.000 → bayar 10.000 × 1.42 = Rp 14.200.
Total dibayar: 13.300 + 14.200 = Rp 27.500.
Konversi decimal: 1 + (1 ÷ |odd|) → -1.33 = 1.75.

RUMUS TARUHAN BERGANDA (MIX PARLAY)

✅ MENANG FULLOdds tetap.
⚡ MENANG 1/2((Odds − 1) ÷ 2) + 1.
⚠️ KALAH 1/2Odds × 0.50.
🤝 SERI / VOIDOdds = 1.00.
📌 TOTALSemua odds efektif dikalikan, lalu × modal.

CONTOH PERHITUNGAN LENGKAP

MENANG FULL

modal: rp 100.000 tim 1: 1.80 (menang full) tim 2: 1.70 (menang full) tim 3: 1.85 (menang full) total odds = 1.80 × 1.70 × 1.85 = 5.661 total hasil = 5.661 × 100.000 = rp 566.100 kemenangan = 566.100 − 100.000 = rp 466.100

MENANG 1/2

modal: rp 100.000 tim 1: 1.80 (menang 1/2) → ((1.80−1)÷2)+1 = 1.40 tim 2: 1.70 (menang full) tim 3: 1.85 (menang full) total odds = 1.40 × 1.70 × 1.85 = 4.403 total hasil = rp 440.300 kemenangan = rp 340.300

KALAH 1/2
